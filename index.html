Ingredients

SodaSlim contains a combination of botanical extracts and nutritional ingredients selected to complement weight management, metabolism, appetite awareness, and daily energy. Each ingredient provides a different type of nutritional or botanical support.

  1. Green Tea Extract - Green Tea Extract comes from the leaves of Camellia sinensis and naturally contains plant compounds known as catechins. It is commonly included in dietary supplements focused on metabolic wellness and everyday energy support.
  2. Green Coffee Bean Extract - Green Coffee Bean Extract is obtained from unroasted coffee beans and naturally contains chlorogenic acids. It is frequently used in weight-management supplements intended to complement healthy nutrition and regular physical activity.
  3. Garcinia Cambogia Extract - Garcinia Cambogia is a tropical fruit whose rind contains hydroxycitric acid (HCA). It is commonly included in dietary supplements focused on appetite awareness and weight-management routines.
  4. Raspberry Ketones - Raspberry Ketones are naturally occurring aromatic compounds found in raspberries. They are commonly used in nutritional formulas designed to complement metabolic and weight-wellness routines.
  5. Caffeine Anhydrous - Caffeine Anhydrous is a concentrated form of caffeine commonly used to support alertness, focus, and daily energy. Because caffeine can affect individuals differently, total daily caffeine intake and product directions should be considered.
